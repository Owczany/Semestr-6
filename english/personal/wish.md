WISH / IF ONLY

## **PRESENT** -> PAST SIMPLE / PAST CONTINUOUS

```
I don't have a beard.

        |
        V

I **WISH** I had a beard. - Chciałbym mieć brodę
```

## PAST -> PAST PERFECT
```
I didn't have a beard when I was a boy.


        |
        V

I **WISH** I had had a beard when I was a boy.

Sometimes I **WISH** I'd never been born.

```
## FUTURE -> WOULD
```
I hope you will have a beard soon.

        |
        V

I **WISH** you would have a beard soon.


I **WISH** you would stop smoking!

I **WISH** it would rain now.
```
